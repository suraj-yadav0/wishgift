# Brag Plan: WishGift

## What is this app?
WishGift is a full-stack wishlist application where users curate gift ideas and friends can secretly reserve items so no one gets duplicate or unwanted presents.

## The angle
Gift-giving is broken: people get duplicate toasters, unwanted sweaters, or have to awkwardly coordinate in secret group chats. WishGift solves this with private wishlists, follower approvals, and secret gift reservations.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"No more unwanted presents or awkward duplicates." A bold, warm entrance introducing WishGift.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Wishlist Builder: A clean UI mockup of "Birthday Wishlist 2026" with priority tags and pricing.
- The Secret Reservation: A friend clicks "Reserve Gift" — it updates in real time to prevent duplicates while keeping the recipient in suspense.

## Outro / punchline
"Thoughtful gifting. Zero awkwardness." WishGift logo, tech stack callout (Next.js 16, Tailwind CSS v4, Prisma), and CTA.

## User flow worth showing
1. Create a wishlist: Add gift items with priority and price tags.
2. Share with friends: Approved followers can browse your public wishlists.
3. Reserve in secret: Friends coordinate reservations without spoiling the surprise.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: Warm, delightful product launch
- Interpretation: Confident pacing, warm color palette, clean typography, and motion that demonstrates real utility.

## Audio direction
- Role: Warm, upbeat background bed with tasteful UI clicks, card drops, and an achievement bell.
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3
- Music treatment: Starts at t=0s at 0.35 volume, continues through scenes, smoothly fades out over the last 1.5 seconds.
- Music cue guidance: 120.19 BPM track. Major transitions land at ~4.5s, 9.0s, and 14.0s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; card shadow and warm hero glow breathe slightly with the music bed.
- SFX posture: Sparse, motion-matched UI accents.
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - 0.3s: Soft drop sound for the welcome badge.
  - 5.5s, 6.5s, 7.5s: Card slide/drop sounds as wishlist items appear.
  - 10.5s: Mouse click sound as "Reserve Gift" is selected.
  - 11.0s: Resonant bell/chime as the reservation status confirms.
- Restraint rule: No harsh buzzers, no rapid-fire clicks. Keep volumes balanced.
